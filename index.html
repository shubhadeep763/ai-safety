Why AI safety

Over the past year I've moved from physics into evaluating frontier AI systems, first as a benchmark author and now as a red-teamer. Both jobs have the same core task: work out what a model can actually be trusted to do, and build tests whose results can't be argued with.

Red-teaming models in Assamese made one question concrete for me: if safety behaviour is trained and measured mostly in English, how much of it carries over to the languages that most of the world speaks? Authoring physics benchmarks taught me how easy it is to write a test that seems hard but measures the wrong thing, and how much work goes into making a failure unambiguous.

I want to understand the wider picture of how advanced AI could go wrong and which interventions matter: evaluations, governance, and the strategic choices labs and states face. I also want to work out where someone with my background can contribute most.
